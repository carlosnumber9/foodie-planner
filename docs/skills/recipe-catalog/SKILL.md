# Maintain catalog recipes

Read [the shared catalog authoring workflow](../../catalog-authoring.md), then inspect relevant recipes, foods, units, and schemas. Codex may propose a recipe when asked; show per-serving quantities, ordered steps, and referenced food revisions before acceptance.

Resolve missing foods through the food catalog workflow; do not create unapproved ingredients or assume local browser units exist in the catalog. Calculate nutrition from pinned foods, never author recipe totals manually. Accepted edits append revisions; archive recipes without deleting references. Follow shared validation and reporting steps. Content acceptance never includes commit, push, or publication.
